The Collegeville Project is focused on exploring new research topics in computational science and engineering (CSE) while at the same time exploring new research methodologies as part of scope.  I led the project primarily in my former role as Scientist in Residence at St. John's University, MN, before retiring from teaching in 2026.  Most of the research is conducted by undergraduate computer science, mathematics and, recently, psychology students.  The primary content from my teaching career, including the CS373 capstone course, is now archived at [Corpus](https://collegeville.github.io/Corpus).
